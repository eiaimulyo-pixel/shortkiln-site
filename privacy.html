Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

This policy covers the website shortkiln.com, operated by ShortKiln.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies of its own and no analytics or advertising trackers (see Embedded videos below). ShortKiln does not collect personal information through this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its domain is served through Cloudflare DNS. These providers may log technical data such as IP addresses as part of operating their services, under their own privacy policies.

Embedded videos

The home page embeds videos from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play one, YouTube may collect data under Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you email hello@shortkiln.com, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We do not sell or share them. Mail to this address is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
